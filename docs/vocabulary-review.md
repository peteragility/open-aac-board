# Starter vocabulary review (draft, 2026-11-03)

The starter vocabulary grew from 51 words and 12 phrases to 231 words and 32 phrases. Existing words keep their positions; new words were appended after them in each category, so nobody's board moves. This is a draft written without a speech-language therapist. Before everyday reliance, a qualified therapist and native Hong Kong Cantonese speakers should review the labels, Jyutping and pictures below.

## Please check first

- **Jyutping that differs between colloquial and reading pronunciations.** Colloquial Hong Kong readings were chosen, e.g. 雞蛋 gai1 daan2, 魚 jyu2, 燒賣 siu1 maai2, 的士 dik1 si2, 巴士 baa1 si2, 公園 gung1 jyun2, 哥哥 go4 go1, 姐姐 ze4 ze1, 妹妹 mui4 mui2, 爺爺 je4 je2, 聽日 ting1 jat6, 上面 soeng6 min6, 下面 haa6 min6, 點樣 dim2 joeng2, 嬲 nau1, 咳 kat1, 考試 haau2 si5, 畫畫 waak6 waa2.
- **Word choices.** Everyday Hong Kong words were preferred: 提子 (grapes), 鉸剪 (scissors), 想攬 (want a hug), 雪糕, 汽水, 茶餐廳. 唔好 is labelled "not good" in English; it also means "don't" in Cantonese.
- **Grammar words** (end of Core words): 咗 緊 過 嘅 呀 啦 (Cantonese and Mandarin 了 着 过 的 啊 吧) and 喇 咩 㗎 (Cantonese only). They are hidden on English (and, for the last three, Mandarin) boards and never spoken in another language. Mandarin 着 for 緊 keeps the Cantonese word order (verb + marker).
- **Pictures marked approximate** below are the closest bundled symbol, not an exact match. 27 simple original pictograms were added (character tiles for grammar and question words).
